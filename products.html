Why they don't fall apart

Sealed, stitched, and stress-tested by actual kids

Every pair is filled and sealed by hand, then double-stitched round the edge so nothing works loose. Before anything goes on this site, it gets dropped, kicked, and generally abused by the youngest testers we could find — free of charge, because they thought it was fun.

If something ever does split at the seam, tell us and we'll sort it. That's not a policy we copied from somewhere, it's just what we'd want if it were our kid.
